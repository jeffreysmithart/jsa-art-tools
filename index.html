    Color space

    Load an image to see its colors in the color space.

    Each dot is a color in the picture, bigger where it covers more.

    How to read this

    It’s laid out like the Munsell color solid: value runs up the middle from black to white, hue goes around (R, YR, Y, GY, G, BG, B, PB, P, RP), and chroma gets stronger away from the gray center. Tap the view to open it large, turn it, and see one value at a time.

    Positions come from CIE Lab, so hue and chroma are close to Munsell, not exact. Squint (in Study) smooths out photo noise first.

    The ringed dots are the main colors.

    Limited palette

    The faint cloud is what the palette can mix; faded dots are colors in the picture it can’t.

    How mixing is worked out

    Mixes are simulated with spectral.js (Kubelka–Munk: how paint absorbs and scatters light) from typical colors of each paint, up to three at a time, with white and black where the palette has them. Brands vary, so treat it as a guide.

    “Can mix” means within ΔE 10, a small but visible difference.
